ABOUT

I LIKE THINGS THAT MAKE SENSE TOGETHER.

I'm Gianmarco, a visual and editorial designer with a background in Fashion Design and Communication.

I approach design as a system rather than a collection of isolated outputs. From publications to identities, I connect content, imagery and typography to build a coherent visual language across different formats and touchpoints.

I'm particularly drawn to independent and responsible businesses, where design can give structure to ideas and make communication clearer and more intentional.
